SmartMixer installer firmware and programming tools

Arduino AVR Boards 1.8.6
The bundled firmware is compiled against Arduino AVR Boards 1.8.6, distributed under GPL-2.0-or-later.
The corresponding SmartMixer sketch is included in firmware\SmartMixerProMicro.ino.
Arduino AVR core source: https://github.com/arduino/ArduinoCore-avr/tree/1.8.6

AVRDUDE 6.3.0-arduino17
The bundled programmer is the AVRDUDE build distributed by Arduino for its AVR platform.
AVRDUDE source and license: https://github.com/arduino/avrdude

libusb-win32
The bundled libusb0.dll is distributed with Arduino's AVRDUDE package.
Source and license information: https://github.com/mcuee/libusb-win32
